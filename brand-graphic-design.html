Graphic Design Services Malaysia

Company profiles, brand materials, presentations and social media artwork for Malaysian businesses.

Request Consultation →All Services →

What we’ll discuss

  • Brand identity and consistency
  • Design for a specific audience
  • Prepare screen and print artwork
  • Review and hand over

Brand identity and consistency

Start with where the design will be used. A company profile, sales deck and social campaign should share recognisable typography, colours and image direction.

Design for a specific audience

For a company profile, organise services, credentials and contact details around buyer questions. For social graphics, agree the message, placement and dimensions before creating a set.

Prepare screen and print artwork

Specify the final size, file type, image quality and whether editable artwork is needed. Print jobs also depend on the supplier’s bleed and colour requirements.

Review and hand over

Provide the approved copy, logos and imagery. We agree concepts, review rounds, final outputs and any editable files in the quotation.

Design materials for business communication

Visual identity and brand application

Develop or refine how your business presents itself across logos, colours, typography and layouts. Agree which elements need a guide for consistent use by your team.

Company profiles and presentations

Structure the story around what you do, who you serve and what a customer can ask for next. Adapt layouts for print, email PDFs or presentation screens where required.

Campaign and social artwork

Prepare visuals for announcements, service promotions and events. Agree the message, call to action, dimensions and export formats for each channel.

What we need to start

Share your current logo, approved wording, photos or references, and where the design will appear. Identify who approves copy and who consolidates revisions.

What to request at handover

Ask for the agreed screen and print exports, source files where included, and a list of assets that have licence or usage restrictions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work with an existing logo?

Share the best source file and brand guide you have. The brief can cover applying or refining an identity, depending on scope.

What should feedback include?

Collect your team’s comments together and point to the exact text, image or placement to change.

Do you provide editable files?

Ask for the required format at quotation stage. Image licences, fonts and editable source files can have separate terms.
