Hostel allocation workflow example

In a worker accommodation system, staff need to know who is staying where and which beds are available. This example is based on a hostel management workflow developed for accommodation operations. The sample records below are fictional.

  1. Check availability. Start from a hostel and select a bed that is available for the move date. Confirm the person’s current assignment before making a change.
  2. Record the transfer. A transfer ends the old allocation and begins the new allocation. The two changes should succeed together to avoid leaving the worker with two active beds or no active bed.
  3. Review the result. Test that the destination is occupied, the previous bed has been released, and staff can distinguish a transfer from a move offsite.
Try a sample move

Sample worker W-014 occupies bed A-12 and needs to move to B-08. If B-08 is already occupied, preserve W-014’s current assignment and ask staff to select another bed. If B-08 is available and the transfer succeeds, W-014 occupies B-08 and A-12 becomes available. The number of assigned workers in this move stays the same.
