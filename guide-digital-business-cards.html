How Digital Business Cards Work

A digital business card is a shareable web profile that can make professional contact details easier to revisit.

All Nuvix guides

Prepare a profile

Choose the contact information you want to publish. Check the mobile layout, links and how recipients can save or reuse details. Share only details intended for public view.

Example

At an event, a representative can place the profile URL on a badge QR code and send the same link in a follow-up email.

Try a sample contact profile

This fictional profile demonstrates what to check when someone opens a digital business card. Choose an action to see where it would lead in a real profile.

Check the business details

Example profile for Aina Tan, business development at Example Studio. On your own card, check the spelling, job title and whether the details make sense to someone who has just met you.

Check a contact action

On a real card, a call action should open the dialler with your business number. An email action should open a draft to the correct address. Test each route before distributing the link.

Check what the recipient can save

If your card offers a contact download, open it and inspect the contact fields before saving. Check which details go into the address book and whether a business website or email is missing.
