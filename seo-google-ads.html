SEO and paid search solve different problems

SEO improves pages so they can be discovered and understood in organic search. Google Ads pays for campaign exposure and can test demand for a defined offer while a campaign is active.

Match search intent to the page

Separate people researching options from people ready to request a quotation. Align each service page or campaign with the question, location and offer it actually serves.

Review technical and on-page signals

Check titles, internal links, crawl rules, canonicals and mobile usability. Use Search Console to review indexing and search queries where access is available.

Plan campaigns and measure enquiries

Agree budget, geography, keywords, exclusions and landing pages. Define meaningful conversions, check tracking and report qualified enquiries alongside clicks and spend.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take?

Indexing and changes in visibility vary with the site, competition and search demand. Agree a review schedule using available data.

Do I need both services?

SEO may suit ongoing discovery. Paid search may suit a time-sensitive campaign or offer test. Choose based on your budget, goals and ability to measure leads.

Are rankings or enquiries guaranteed?

Search rankings and advertising results vary. Reporting should show what happened and what can be tested next.
