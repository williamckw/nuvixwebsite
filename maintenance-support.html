Website Maintenance and Support Malaysia

Website support for Malaysian businesses covering updates, troubleshooting, backups and coordination with hosting providers.

Request Consultation →All Services →

What we’ll discuss

  • Define what needs ongoing care
  • Update, back up and verify
  • Troubleshoot with a clear record
  • Agree scope and escalation

Define what needs ongoing care

List who owns the site, domain, hosting and software accounts. Identify pages or forms that matter most, along with the access needed to support them.

Update, back up and verify

For WordPress, review plugin and theme changes and have a rollback plan before updating. Agree backup responsibility and test that critical pages and enquiry paths still work afterwards.

Troubleshoot with a clear record

Record the affected URL, when the issue started and any recent changes. Check logs and hosting or DNS settings where access is available.

Agree scope and escalation

Separate routine updates from urgent recovery, redesigns and third-party incidents. Define what is included, what needs a separate quote and who approves changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you support my existing host?

Share the hosting provider and access available. Support depends on the account permissions and the service’s capabilities.

What should I do if a page breaks?

Send the URL, a screenshot and the time it occurred. Mention recent changes so the issue can be reproduced.

Are uptime and security guaranteed?

Hosting, software and third-party services affect uptime and security. Agree monitoring, backups and escalation responsibilities for the site.
