Frequently Asked Questions

What does Nuvix do?

Nuvix builds websites and custom systems for Malaysian businesses and supports automation, digital identity, marketing and design.

Should I start with a website or a system?

Start with a website if customers need to find and contact you. Start with a system if staff need to share records or follow an internal process.

Can you improve an existing website?

Share the URL, editing access and what needs to improve. The first review can cover content, navigation, enquiry paths and technical issues.

What affects a project quote?

Scope, page count, integrations, content readiness, review rounds and ongoing support affect the quotation.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Tell us what your business does, what you want to improve, who needs to use it and when you need it.
