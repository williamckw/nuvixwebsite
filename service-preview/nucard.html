NuCard Digital Business Card Malaysia

NuCard is Nuvix’s digital business card for professionals and Malaysian businesses. Share a professional profile after meetings, events or sales visits so people can find your business details and get back in touch.

Explore NuCard →Ask about NuCard →

What a digital name card does

A digital name card gives someone a web address they can revisit after a meeting. A profile can bring together the contact details and links a person chooses to share.

Share by link or QR code

A QR code is a shortcut to a web address. A printed card or event badge can carry a QR code, while a link works in an email or chat. Test the resulting page on a phone before sharing widely.

Printed cards and NFC

Printed cards are familiar and work as physical handouts. An NFC tag can open a URL when a compatible phone reads it. Ask which sharing options are currently available with NuCard before ordering hardware.

Use it for yourself or a team

Individuals can decide what information to publish. A company should decide which details each team member shares and who will make changes when someone joins or leaves.

Give new contacts a simple way to find you again

After a meeting or networking event

Share a profile link in your follow-up so the other person can revisit your business details when they are ready to get in touch. Use the same destination on printed material if you choose to add a QR code.

For individuals and business owners

Put the details you want to share in one place, such as a business name, website and preferred contact channel. Choose a clear next step so someone can easily enquire or learn about your services.

For sales teams and organisations

Plan a consistent presentation for representatives. Before rollout, decide which contact details should be public, who will maintain them and which team features you need to confirm with NuCard.

See how NuCard fits your business

Open the NuCard website to explore the product. If you are preparing for an event or ordering for your team, tell us how many people will use it and how they prefer to share their details.

A practical networking example

A business owner can include a profile link in a follow-up message and print a QR code pointing to the same destination on an event card. Test both routes on a phone before sending the artwork to print.
