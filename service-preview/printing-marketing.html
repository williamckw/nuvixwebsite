Choose the piece and its purpose

A flyer fits a single offer, while a brochure explains several services. An event banner needs a short message sized for its viewing distance. A business card needs readable details at hand-held size.

Prepare artwork for production

Confirm final dimensions, bleed, safe margins, image resolution and the supplier’s required PDF and colour settings before export.

Select quantities and finishing

Give the intended use, quantity and deadline. Ask which paper, finishing and production options are available for the job and who will fulfil it.

Proof before ordering

Check spelling, contact details, logo quality, QR scanning and dimensions. Approve the proof and confirm production and delivery arrangements before printing.

Print and marketing materials by use

Business and sales materials

Plan name cards, flyers, brochures and company profile materials around what recipients need to keep or act on. Decide whether you also need a lightweight digital version for messaging.

Events and point-of-sale

Translate your campaign into banners, posters or display graphics. Specify viewing distance, installation area, quantity and delivery dates before artwork begins.

Artwork and proofing

Check dimensions, bleed, logo quality and colour requirements with the printer. Review a proof for text, contact information and QR scannability before sign-off.

What to include in a quote request

Send the finished size, quantity, intended use, artwork status and date required. Ask which paper, finishes and delivery options are available for the job.

Who handles production

Confirm the supplier, proof approval contact and delivery arrangements in the quotation. Production details depend on the material, quantity and deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I supply my own artwork?

Yes, share the source and export if available. Check whether artwork adjustments or a new proof are included in the quotation.

Do I need a separate digital version?

Usually yes. A print PDF can contain bleed and production settings, while a digital version can use smaller files and clickable links.

Can you confirm lead time?

Lead time depends on proof approval, material availability, quantity and fulfilment. Check the current schedule when requesting a quote.
