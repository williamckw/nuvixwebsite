Choosing the right website

A corporate website gives buyers several pages to explore your company, services and credentials. A landing page focuses on one campaign and one action, such as requesting a quote. A web application handles tasks like signing in, managing records or approving requests.

Planning content and navigation

We map what each visitor needs to know before contacting you. A service page can explain the scope, service area and next steps, while its contact link leads to the right enquiry channel.

WordPress or static HTML

WordPress suits teams that need to edit pages regularly. Static HTML is an option for sites with infrequent updates and fewer publishing needs. We agree on editing access, hosting and maintenance before building.

Design, build and testing

We agree the page list, build responsive layouts and review headings, internal links and on-page SEO. Before launch, check navigation, enquiry links and the layout on phone, tablet and desktop.

What we can build

Corporate and service websites

Give buyers a clear path from your homepage to individual services, relevant examples and a quotation request. A multilingual site can use separate pages for each language when translated content is available.

Campaign landing pages

Build a page around one offer, one audience and a specific action. A campaign for event banners, for example, can answer questions about sizes and artwork before asking for a quote.

Website redesign and migration

Review what visitors use today and carry over important pages, content and assets. If URLs must change, map redirects and test them before switching the domain.

What you can expect at handover

Agree on page layouts, editable sections, access to accounts and a short handover guide. Discuss how enquiries, analytics and future updates will be handled.

A website brief you can use

List your main services and the three questions customers ask before buying. Mark what content you already have and identify the person responsible for approvals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I prepare?

Share your services, target customers, logo, existing domain, approved photos and examples of enquiries you want to receive.

What can the quotation include?

Agree page count, content responsibilities, design revisions, redirects, hosting and support before work begins.

Which approach fits a paid campaign?

A dedicated landing page can keep the offer and next step aligned with the ad. A corporate site gives prospects space to research your wider business.
