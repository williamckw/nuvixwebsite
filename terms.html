Read the Nuvix website terms and learn how quotations, project scope, revisions and deliverables are agreed.

Using this website

The website explains Nuvix services and helps you prepare an enquiry. A service description is general information. A project starts under an agreed quotation or service agreement.

Quotations, scope and approvals

Confirm the deliverables, responsibilities, acceptance criteria and estimated schedule in writing. Changes to scope, revisions, approvals or missing materials can affect cost and timing and should be agreed before extra work begins.

Payment, cancellation and changes

Confirm payment milestones, invoicing, revision limits and cancellation or refund terms in your quotation or project agreement.

Intellectual property and deliverables

The project agreement should identify editable files, source code, usage rights and any transfer terms. Third-party assets, plugins and fonts may have separate licence terms.

Hosting, external services and maintenance

Confirm who owns and pays for domain, hosting and platform accounts and who handles renewals. Third-party terms can apply. Maintenance and incident response depend on the agreed support scope.

Project-specific terms

Confirm liability, applicable law, dispute handling and any warranties in the project agreement. For service or website questions, email nuvixent@gmail.com.
